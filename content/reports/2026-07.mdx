# University AI Policy Dataset Month-End Report: July 2026

Release: `public-release-20260801-001`
Period: July 2026 through 31 July
Published: 2 August 2026

## What changed in this release

This month-end release adds current official AI-tools evidence for Michigan State University and Ulsan National Institute of Science and Technology (UNIST). The public dataset contains 828 university records, 5,476 source-backed claims, 3,195 official source attributions, and 5,741 evidence records in this release snapshot.

Michigan State's current [AI Tools page](https://tech.msu.edu/technology/ai/tools/) documents enterprise-licensed Microsoft Copilot, Microsoft 365 Copilot, ChatGPT EDU, Google Gemini, Google Gemini Notebook, Zoom AI Companion, and developer tools including the OpenAI API Platform, Google Cloud Platform, and Codex Local. The page also states access, purchasing, data-category, and enterprise-protection conditions for the listed tools.

UNIST's current [AI Services page](https://ai.unist.ac.kr/) presents UNIAI as an in-house-developed AI chatbot service and states in Korean that Copilot can be used in MS Office 365 after license allocation. The current page does not display the older “service ends after July 31, 2026” sentence; this release records current availability evidence without inferring that UNIST extended the service end date.

## Review and evidence boundary

- All new claims are `agent_reviewed` and remain source-bound tracker metadata.
- Original-language evidence is canonical; the Korean UNIST snippets retain their source language and use English only as display helpers.
- Tool records are derived discovery metadata, not official university statements.
- Official university pages remain authoritative for institutional language.
- The tracker is not legal advice, academic integrity advice, or course-permission advice.
