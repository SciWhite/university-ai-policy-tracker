# University AI Policy Dataset Month-End Monitoring Report: August 2026

Dataset snapshot: `public-release-20260801-001`
Period: August 2026 through 31 August
Published: 4 September 2026

## Monthly result

No new university policy-content update passed the evidence, review, artifact,
and release-validation gates during August 2026. The latest versioned public
dataset therefore remains `public-release-20260801-001`, containing 828
university records, 5,476 source-backed claims, 3,195 official source
attributions, and 5,741 evidence records.

This is a publication-gate result, not a claim that every university made no
policy changes. Monitoring-only signals, access failures, blocked pages,
metadata changes, and source hashes without comparable old and new policy text
are not published as policy updates.

## Monitoring and review boundary

- Only confirmed policy-sentence changes supported by official source URLs and
  reviewable old/new evidence may become release candidates.
- Routine maintenance metadata does not replace claim evidence and is not
  published as a canonical policy conclusion.
- Inconclusive sources remain held back for later comparison or human review.
- Original-language evidence is canonical; translations are display helpers.
- Official university pages remain authoritative for institutional language.
- The tracker is not legal advice, academic integrity advice, or
  course-permission advice.
