# University of Pennsylvania evidence correction and shared V6 integration

Penn's community guidance, instructor-specific coursework rules, licensed-tool conditions and Wharton Academy policies have different scopes. This release corrects overbroad unlicensed-tool wording, clarifies the authority of the transparency guidance, adds the Penn Center for Innovation consultation path, refreshes current tool evidence and removes tool-only assessment derivations. The 31 original Claim IDs remain; one instructor-investigation guidance record and three currently listed tools bring the record to 35 claims and 12 tools. Prior versions and original evidence are retained.

Penn joins the existing shared compact V6 composition with a verified central-homepage link. No school-specific page component or illustrations are introduced. The fourteen inherited schools' policy records, dates, tools and snapshot dispositions are preserved. Penn remains PARTIAL; no strong student snapshot, complete institutional audit, authenticated entitlement test or formal disciplinary/appeal audit is claimed.

The external audit supplied investigation leads. Its suggested blanket downgrade of all ISC instructions was not adopted: the transparency paragraph uses direct instructions, while the document explicitly states it is not final policy. Grammarly is still listed by CETLI; absence from the ISC directory does not establish withdrawal, and its current price remains unconfirmed. Old names and historical snapshots remain in the frozen baseline. Wharton Academy restrictions stay within that program.

Candidate acceptance and production cutover are pending. This document will be updated only with actual results.
