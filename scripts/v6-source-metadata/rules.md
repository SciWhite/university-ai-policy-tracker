Rules for document metadata of university AI-policy sources (shared by the extract and verify prompts).

- Every non-null value needs "quote": a short span (ideally under 200 characters) copied character-for-character from THIS document's TEXT, not from the header lines, that clearly supports the value. If the text does not state it, use null or an empty list. Never infer from the URL, a title label, your own knowledge or another document. When unsure, leave it out.
- docType: judge from the text. Use tool_listing for pages that list AI tools or services. stage is draft only if the text says draft, proposed or consultation; issued if it reads as an official published document; otherwise unknown.
- issuer: the specific office or body that owns or publishes THIS document. Use role owner when the text names an owner or responsible office, approver when it names the approving body, otherwise publisher_unit for the unit named in the page header, footer or site name (for example "University Library", "Centre for Teaching and Learning"). Prefer the specific unit over the bare university name whenever the text names one. Not issuers: publication or collection names ("Newsletter", "Student Handbook", "Course Catalog"); vendors or tool makers (on a tool page the maker is not the issuer); offices only mentioned, linked or given as contacts; units from unrelated parts of the page; portal or site brand names; site-wide footer lines such as "Authorised by: Director, Marketing"; a unit that only reposted another body's document (use the original author when the text names it). Pick the closest category; communications_or_news for news offices, legal_or_compliance for legal or compliance offices, other only when nothing fits.
- approvedBy: the body or office that approved, adopted or authorised THIS document, only when the text says so ("Approved by: Vice-Chancellor", "Approving authority: Academic Board", "经校长办公会审议通过", "beschlossen vom Senat"). null otherwise.
- dates must be about THIS document. Kinds: effective, approved, adopted, issued_or_published, revised, reviewed (a past review: "Last reviewed", "Reviewed: Feb 2025"), last_updated_page (page "last updated/modified"), next_review (a scheduled future review). A date in a PDF header, footer or cover next to the document title (for example "January 2026") counts as issued_or_published at month precision. A bare date counts only when its meaning is explicit. Value as YYYY-MM-DD, YYYY-MM or YYYY with matching precision. Remove: academic-year or term labels ("2026/27", "AY 2025-26", "Fall 2025", "Semester 1 2026"); dates of another document that is embedded, quoted, linked or listed; event dates, deadlines and news dates about something else; copyright years; ambiguous labels.
- version: an explicit version or revision number only.
- scope: who or what the document applies to, at most 15 English words, with a quote.
